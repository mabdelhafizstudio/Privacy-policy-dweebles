Dweebles · The Holdout

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Last updated: November 3, 2026

The short version: Dweebles: The Holdout does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts of our own.

The game uses Apple's Game Center for sign-in and online co-op. Your game settings and best round are saved only on your device.

1. Who we are

Dweebles: The Holdout (the "Game") is developed and published by MOUD Labs ("we," "us," or "our"), an independent developer based in Kentucky, United States. This policy explains what information the Game handles and how.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not operate servers for the Game, and we do not receive any data from it. Specifically, the Game does not:

  • Ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software from any third party.
  • Use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Access your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
  • Offer in-app purchases or process payments.

3. Information stored on your device

The Game saves a small amount of information locally on your device using standard iOS storage, so your preferences are there next time you play:

  • Gameplay and control settings, such as look sensitivity, invert-Y, field of view, aim assist, haptics, control size and opacity, and graphics options.
  • Audio settings, such as master, sound-effect, and music volume.
  • Your best round reached.

This information never leaves your device through the Game, and we cannot see it. It may be included in your device backups (such as iCloud Backup) according to your own Apple settings. Deleting the Game removes it.

4. Apple Game Center

The Game uses Apple's Game Center to sign you in and to run online co-op matches. Game Center is a service provided and operated by Apple, not by us.

Sign-in

When you open the Game, iOS may sign you in to Game Center automatically. The Game then receives your Game Center display name (for example, to show "Signed in as …" on the main menu) and a game-specific player identifier that Apple assigns. The Game uses these only while it is running and does not send them to us.

Online co-op

If you choose Co-op, Game Center matches you with 2 to 4 players, either friends you invite or other players. During a match, the following is exchanged between the players' devices through Game Center:

WhatWhy
Your Game Center display name and player identifierTo show who is in the match and assign player slots.
Gameplay data, such as movement, aim direction, shots, purchases, revives, points, and healthTo keep the game in sync for everyone in the match.

This data is used only to run the match in real time. It is not stored by the Game after the match ends, and we do not receive it. The Game has no text or voice chat.

Apple handles your Game Center account, friends, invitations, and matchmaking under its own terms. To learn how Apple handles that information, see Apple's Privacy Policy. You can manage or turn off Game Center in your device's Settings. You can still play Solo without Game Center.

5. Game controllers

If you connect a game controller, the Game reads its button and stick input to control your character. That input is used only on your device and is not stored or shared.

6. Links to other websites

The Game's credits include links to outside websites, such as the Sketchfab page of a 3D model's creator and the Creative Commons license. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this policy does not cover them.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the Game does not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to hold about a child. If you believe a child has provided us with information some other way, such as by email, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

Since we do not collect or hold your personal information, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. You remain in control:

  • Change or reset in-game settings at any time in Settings.
  • Delete the Game to remove all locally saved data.
  • Manage your Game Center profile, friends, and visibility in iOS Settings.

If you live somewhere with privacy laws that give you specific rights (such as the EU, UK, or California), those rights apply in full. We simply have no personal data about you on file. If you email us, we use your email only to reply and delete it when it's no longer needed.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future update changes what the Game collects (for example, adding leaderboards, achievements, or in-app purchases), we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above before that update is released.
