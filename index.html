Dweebles · The Holdout

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026 · Last updated: November 14, 2026

The short version: Dweebles: The Holdout does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts of our own.

Co-op uses Apple's Game Center online, or your local network for players nearby. Co-op has proximity voice chat: if you allow the microphone, your voice is sent live to the other players in your match and is never recorded or sent to us. Your settings, records, and nearby name are saved only on your device.

1. Who we are

Dweebles: The Holdout (the "Game"), shown on your device as "Dweebles," is developed and published by MOUD Labs ("we," "us," or "our"), an independent developer based in Kentucky, United States. This policy explains what information the Game handles and how.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not operate servers for the Game, and we do not receive any data from it. Specifically, the Game does not:

  • Ask you to create an account or provide your email address or phone number.
  • Include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking software from any third party.
  • Use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Access your location, contacts, photos, or camera.
  • Record or store audio from your microphone.
  • Offer in-app purchases or process payments.

4. Information stored on your device

The Game saves a small amount of information locally on your device using standard iOS storage, so your preferences are there next time you play:

  • Gameplay and control settings, such as look sensitivity, invert-Y, field of view, aim assist, haptics and controller vibration, control size and opacity, difficulty, game mode, and graphics options.
  • Audio settings, such as master, sound-effect, music, and voice volume, and whether voice chat and your microphone are turned on.
  • Your best rounds, per map, difficulty, and mode.
  • Your last selected maps and the survivor look you chose for co-op.
  • The name you type for nearby co-op, if you enter one.

We cannot see this information. Apart from your nearby name and survivor look, which are shown to the players you join in co-op, it never leaves your device through the Game. It may be included in your device backups (such as iCloud Backup) according to your own Apple settings. Deleting the Game removes it.

5. Co-op multiplayer

Co-op is for 2 to 4 players and works in two ways: online through Apple's Game Center, or directly between devices that are near each other. Solo play needs neither.

Game Center sign-in

When you open the Game, iOS may sign you in to Game Center automatically. Game Center is a service provided and operated by Apple, not by us. The Game receives your Game Center display name (for example, to show "Signed in as …" on the main menu) and a game-specific player identifier that Apple assigns. The Game uses these only while it is running and does not send them to us.

Online co-op

If you play co-op online, Game Center matches you with friends you invite or with other players, and the match data travels through Apple's Game Center service.

Nearby co-op

If you play co-op with players nearby, the Game uses your local network (Wi-Fi or a direct device-to-device link) to find and join lobbies. This needs the Local Network permission.

  • While you host a nearby lobby, your device announces it on the local network under your nearby name, so other devices running the Game nearby can see that name and join.
  • Your nearby name is the name you type in the lobby (up to 20 characters). If you leave it blank, the Game uses your Game Center display name if you are signed in, or "Survivor." Do not use a name you would not want people near you to see.
  • Nearby matches connect the players' devices directly. The data does not pass through Apple's Game Center or any server of ours, and the Game does not add encryption of its own on top of your network's.

6. Voice chat and your microphone

Co-op matches have proximity voice chat: you hear teammates whose characters are near yours in the game, louder the closer they are. Voice chat is on by default in co-op, and it is never used in Solo.

  • Permission. The Game can only use your microphone after you allow it in the iOS prompt. If you decline, you can still play and hear others; they will not hear you.
  • When the microphone is on. The microphone is open only during a co-op match, while voice chat and the Microphone setting are both on. It is not open in menus, in the lobby, in Solo, or after the match ends. There is no push-to-talk: while it is open, what your microphone picks up, including background sound, is sent.
  • Where your voice goes. Your voice is compressed on your device and sent live to the device hosting the match, which plays it and passes it on to the players whose characters are within earshot of yours. Online, it travels through Apple's Game Center service; in nearby co-op, it goes directly between devices.
  • No recording. The Game does not record, save, transcribe, or analyze voice audio, and we never receive it. It is played once on the other players' devices and discarded.
  • Other players. People in your match can hear what you say, and we cannot control what they do with it, such as recording it with their own equipment. In online matches you may be playing with people you do not know.

To control voice chat, open the Game's Settings ▸ Audio: turn off Microphone to stop sending your voice while still hearing others, or turn off Proximity voice chat to switch it off entirely. You can also withdraw microphone access for the Game in iOS Settings at any time.

7. Game controllers

If you connect a game controller, the Game reads its button and stick input to control your character and may send vibration to it. That input is used only on your device and is not stored or shared.

9. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the Game does not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to hold about a child. Co-op lets players talk to each other by voice, including people they do not know in online matches; a parent or guardian can turn voice chat off in the Game's Settings, deny the microphone permission, or restrict Game Center multiplayer with Screen Time. If you believe a child has provided us with information some other way, such as by email, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your choices and rights

Since we do not collect or hold your personal information, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. You remain in control:

  • Change or reset in-game settings, including voice chat and your microphone, at any time in Settings.
  • Change your nearby name in the nearby co-op lobby.
  • Allow or deny Microphone and Local Network access for the Game in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the Game to remove all locally saved data.
  • Manage your Game Center profile, friends, and visibility in iOS Settings.

If you live somewhere with privacy laws that give you specific rights (such as the EU, UK, or California), those rights apply in full. We simply have no personal data about you on file. If you email us, we use your email only to reply and delete it when it's no longer needed.
